TGshka HOS — Privacy Policy

Version 3 · prepared 2 November 2026; applies to the release requesting consent version 3 · previous version 2: 27 October 2026 · Русская версия

TGshka HOS is an unofficial Telegram client for HarmonyOS watches. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Telegram. It talks to Telegram through the official Telegram API (the TDLib library).

This document says what happens to your data when you use the app. The short version: the developer of TGshka HOS has no servers and receives nothing from the app — no account data, no messages, no analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers. Account messages travel between your watch and Telegram. Optional model downloads and configured proxies also contact the recipients described in section 3.

3. Other network connections

Besides Telegram (directly or through a proxy you configured), the app connects to:

  • huggingface.co or its mirror hf-mirror.com — only to download a Whisper dictation model: when you ask for one (Settings → Dictation), or, after acceptance of the current privacy notice, by itself when your dictation language has no model inside the app (only Russian has one, and it is also downloaded for Russian if that model does not run on your watch), none is on the watch yet, and the watch is on its own Wi‑Fi while the app is open — never through your phone. Remove the model or stop that download, and none is downloaded by itself again until you download one yourself. The request contains nothing about you beyond what any download reveals (your IP address).
  • A proxy bridge page — only if you add a proxy of the "web proxy" type; the address is the one you entered.
  • Your phone on the same Wi-Fi network — only while you use "Add proxy from phone": the watch shows a one-time address and code, and the page you open on the phone sends the proxy link to the watch. Nothing leaves your local network. This local HTTP channel is not encrypted: use a trusted Wi-Fi network; someone able to observe that network may see the proxy credentials. A one-time token and confirmation do not provide encryption.

5. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Log out (Settings → Accounts) requests termination of the Telegram session and records local deletion. The account is removed from use; its database, key and files known to belong only to it are deleted after its native client closes safely. If closing or storage fails, the deletion remains pending and is retried at the next launch. This is logical deletion, not guaranteed physical overwriting of flash. If Telegram cannot be reached, local deletion does not confirm server-side revocation: end the remaining session from another device (Telegram Settings → Devices).
  • Clearing the cache (Settings → Data) removes downloaded files.
  • Unsent or unconfirmed recordings are protected from automatic age-based cleanup. A draft's file and account ownership can survive a crash even when its chat-screen draft cannot be restored. Logging out removes that account's recorded ownership after safe closure; a file still owned by another account is retained. Older temporary files without ownership records are not automatically deleted by logout or Clear cache. To erase these retained files as well, use the watch's system control to clear this app's data, if available, or uninstall the app; this also removes the app's other local data and sessions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored.
  • Your Telegram account itself is not affected by any of the above. It is deleted with Telegram, at https://telegram.org/deactivate; the app shows this address under Settings → About → Privacy → Delete Telegram account.

The app takes no part in the system's own backup feature: nothing it stores — not your Telegram session, not the message cache, not its settings — is put into a backup or carried to another device.

6. Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, requests to access, correct, export or erase personal data cannot be answered by the developer — there is nothing to look up. For the data in your Telegram account, use Telegram's own tools and contacts (see Telegram's Privacy Policy). For the data on your watch, use the controls in section 5. If you believe this app handles your data unlawfully you may also complain to the data-protection authority of your country.

7. Children

The app is a client for Telegram and can be used only with a Telegram account. Telegram's Terms of Service set the minimum age for an account; the app is not directed at children below that age.

8. Security

Traffic between the app and Telegram is encrypted by Telegram's MTProto protocol, implemented by the TDLib library. Account data on the watch is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. TDLib's database (the session and the messages) is encrypted with a random key that only this watch's keystore holds, available after the watch's first unlock. When secure storage is unavailable, database opening is blocked with a retryable error. An older database made with TDLib's built-in fixed key is migrated using a random key saved before migration; interruption preserves both opening possibilities until the migration succeeds. Existing legacy settings are retained until secure migration succeeds, but new secret changes are never silently saved in Preferences. Proxy secrets, login codes, passwords and phone numbers are never written to logs.
