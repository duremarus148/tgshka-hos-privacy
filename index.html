TGshka HOS — Privacy Policy

Version 1 · effective 4 October 2026 · revised 21 October 2026 (more precise; nothing more is collected) · Русская версия

TGshka HOS is an unofficial Telegram client for HarmonyOS watches. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Telegram. It talks to Telegram through the official Telegram API (the TDLib library).

This document says what happens to your data when you use the app. The short version: the developer of TGshka HOS has no servers and receives nothing from the app — no account data, no messages, no analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers. Everything the app handles stays on your watch or travels between your watch and Telegram.

3. Other network connections

Besides Telegram (directly or through a proxy you configured), the app connects to:

  • huggingface.co or its mirror hf-mirror.com — only if you ask the app to download an optional Whisper dictation model (Settings → Dictation). The request contains nothing about you beyond what any download reveals (your IP address).
  • A proxy bridge page — only if you add a proxy of the "web proxy" type; the address is the one you entered.
  • Your phone on the same Wi-Fi network — only while you use "Add proxy from phone": the watch shows a one-time address and code, and the page you open on the phone sends the proxy link to the watch. Nothing leaves your local network.

5. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Log out (Settings → Accounts) ends the session with Telegram and erases that account's data from the watch. If the watch cannot reach Telegram at that moment, the data and the session key are erased from the watch all the same (after about half a minute), and the session stays in your Telegram account's list of devices — end it from another device (Telegram Settings → Devices).
  • Clearing the cache (Settings → Data) removes downloaded files.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored.
  • Your Telegram account itself is not affected by any of the above. It is deleted with Telegram, at https://telegram.org/deactivate; the app shows this address under Settings → About → Privacy → Delete Telegram account.

The app takes no part in the system's own backup feature: nothing it stores — not your Telegram session, not the message cache, not its settings — is put into a backup or carried to another device.

6. Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, requests to access, correct, export or erase personal data cannot be answered by the developer — there is nothing to look up. For the data in your Telegram account, use Telegram's own tools and contacts (see Telegram's Privacy Policy). For the data on your watch, use the controls in section 5. If you believe this app handles your data unlawfully you may also complain to the data-protection authority of your country.

7. Children

The app is a client for Telegram and can be used only with a Telegram account. Telegram's Terms of Service set the minimum age for an account; the app is not directed at children below that age.

8. Security

Traffic between the app and Telegram is encrypted by Telegram's MTProto protocol, implemented by the TDLib library. Account data on the watch is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. TDLib's database (the session and the messages) is encrypted with a random key that only this watch's keystore holds, available after the watch's first unlock (a watch whose keystore cannot be used keeps the database with TDLib's own built-in key). Proxy secrets, login codes, passwords and phone numbers are never written to logs.
