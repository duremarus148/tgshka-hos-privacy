Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Bezelcade is an offline arcade game for Huawei watches. This policy covers the game installed on the watch.

Data on the watch

The game stores your selected game, sound and difficulty settings, records, wins and medals in its private storage. The history contains up to 12 completed rounds with the game, difficulty, score, outcome and time. Earlier rounds drop out of the history as you play. Records and settings remain until you clear the data or uninstall the game.

Connections and permissions

The game runs without an account or an internet connection. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not send your settings, results or device identifiers to the publisher or to third parties. The vibration permission provides tactile feedback during play.

Store purchases

AppGallery handles the purchase and download under the terms of your store region. Bezelcade does not request or store your payment details. For purchase history, payment questions or refunds, use AppGallery.

Your data and choices

Open Records on the home screen to view your scores and round history. To erase them, clear Bezelcade’s app data in the watch settings, if this option is available, or uninstall the game. Your locally stored records and settings will be removed. The developer has no server copy to retrieve, correct or export.

Contact the publisher

For a question about the game’s privacy practices, use the developer’s public contact page. Messages posted there may be public. Please omit names, payment information and other personal details.
